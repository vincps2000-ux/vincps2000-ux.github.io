Core Claim: 

The Nuances of a Experimental Setup  have a huge effect on Experiment outcome in CS, and is barely being controlled for.
Because Computer systems are complex and highly interdependent, small changes can have huge effects.
E.g. Changing a single unreelated variable, can have effects like taking up memory, making the experiment non-transferable (pr less-transferable).

Core method:
Survey of 133 recent papers.

Setup:
Tested with  SPEC CPU2006 Benchmark suite(C only),
across Pentium 4 and Core 2 workstation.
