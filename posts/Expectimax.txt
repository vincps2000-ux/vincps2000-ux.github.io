What is Expectimax? 
A Gametheory algorithm, that is based on the Chance of all possible Outcomes, not on Optimnal play.

E.g. when choosing betweent two Options one with Value 100, and one with a Chance for 1000 Points on Enemy misplay, otherwise 99%:
Typical Algorithm assumes optimal play takes the 100. Expectimax calculates ((1000 + 99) / 2) abd chooses risky Option.

Disadvantage: 
Chance of Misplay are often hard to quantify, only really useful against random opponent.
